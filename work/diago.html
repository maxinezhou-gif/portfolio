Overview

A healthcare app with wearable devices for families in the US.

Because of the pandemic, many drawbacks of the healthcare system in the US were exposed. The shortage of medical resources made getting treatment harder for patients, especially those without medical insurance. So I set out to relieve pressure on the US healthcare system and provide a better, quicker diagnosis experience for patients.
